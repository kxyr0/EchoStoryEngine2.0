# Navigation Traversals

Typed traversal values:

- `WALK`
- `STEP_UP`
- `STEP_DOWN`
- `STAIR_UP`
- `STAIR_DOWN`
- `SLAB_UP`
- `SLAB_DOWN`
- `LADDER_UP`
- `LADDER_DOWN`
- `OPEN_DOOR`
- `PASS_DOOR`

Doors are left as-is after passage. Closed wooden doors may be opened by the NPC near the route edge; closed iron doors
do not become passable.
